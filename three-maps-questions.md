# The Three Maps — Reflection Worksheet

Adapted from Chapter 2, “Three Maps,” of Tanya Reilly’s *The Staff Engineer’s Path*. The short anchor questions and spectrum labels follow the chapter; the answer structure and follow-up prompts are formatted as a practical worksheet.

For each answer, try to record:

- **Observation:** What seems true?
- **Evidence:** What specific examples support it?
- **Impact:** How does this affect your work?
- **Response:** How should you adapt, or what should change?
